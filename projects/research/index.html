04 / RESEARCH / EDUCATION TECHNOLOGY

Research into e-learning authoring tools.

A UCL dissertation on designers, software choices and professional practice.

Read the research findings →

PROJECTUCL Institute of Education
ROLEResearch design · data analysis · academic writing
01 / PROBLEM

Examine the tool’s influence

E-learning authoring tools are often treated as neutral containers. I wanted to examine how their features and constraints influence the design decisions practitioners make.

02 / RESPONSE

Survey and interview designers

I designed an online survey, conducted semi-structured interviews with instructional designers and analysed how they chose tools and adapted their work to them.

03 / RESULT

Award-winning dissertation

The dissertation received the Exceptional Dissertation Award from the UCL Institute of Education.

A brief look through the dissertation.

Read a text description of the silent walkthrough

This silent recording scrolls through the dissertation contents. The chapters cover a literature review, methodology, findings, discussion and conclusion.
