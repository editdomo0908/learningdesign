03 / BUSINESS ANALYSIS / DOCUMENTATION

Process mapping and user manual.

Mapped pricing workflows across systems and created a shared user manual for Product, QA and Customer Service.

PROJECTSwiss aviation marketplace
ROLEQA engineer · business analysis support · documentation
01 / PROBLEM

Make pricing understandable

A growing aviation marketplace needed a shared understanding of how its pricing engine behaved across roles and workflows. The feature was complex, and different teams needed information at different levels of detail.

02 / RESPONSE

Map and explain the logic

I investigated system behaviour, worked alongside the Business Analyst, mapped workflows and translated the logic into a structured user manual with diagrams and practical examples.

03 / RESULT

One shared reference

The documentation provided a common reference for Product, Development, QA and Customer Service as the feature evolved.
