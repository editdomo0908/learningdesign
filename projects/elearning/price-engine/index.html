01 / E-LEARNING / ARTICULATE STORYLINE

Price Engine for Customer Service.

A fictional scenario-based course for the Customer Service team, focused on investigating and explaining pricing questions.

Portfolio course · Ready to try

PROJECTCustomer pricing scenario · Published Storyline course
ROLELearning design · Interaction design
01 / PROBLEM

Practice a customer decision

Knowing pricing terms is different from responding well when a customer sees a price they do not understand. Customer Service teams need to decide what to check and how to explain what they find.

02 / RESPONSE

Investigate before choosing a response

The course introduces the pricing flow, checks understanding and then presents a customer pricing question. Learners investigate the case, make response choices and receive feedback through the Storyline interactions.

03 / RESULT

A course ready to explore

The 10-slide course is published for the web and can be tried in its original Articulate Storyline player. It is a portfolio learning project.
