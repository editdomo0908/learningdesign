02 / LEARNING DESIGN / VIDEO

Instructional videos.

Task-focused resources to help internal teams and users navigate a marketplace and a campaign management tool.

PROJECTSwiss aviation marketplace/ Advertising campaign tool
ROLELearning resources · user enablement · video production
01 / PROBLEM

Clarify new workflows

New platform features introduced unfamiliar workflows. Users and support teams needed clear guidance they could return to when completing a task.

02 / RESPONSE

Show each task step by step

I structured and produced instructional videos that broke workflows into steps, using screen examples and concise explanations. The videos complemented written onboarding and support materials.

03 / RESULT

A resource users could revisit

The series became a practical reference for Customer Service and platform users during adoption of new functionality.
